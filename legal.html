Personal data, cookies and audience measurement

This website does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. To know how many people visit it, and which pages, CV downloads and project links are used, it uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly audience-measurement service. GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser, sets no cookie, builds no profile and does not follow you from one website to another. It keeps only aggregated statistics : pages viewed, referring website, browser, operating system, screen width, language and country. Your IP address is not stored : it is used only to determine your country and, for up to 8 hours and only in memory, to avoid counting the same visit twice. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing the audience of this website. For this reason, no cookie banner is displayed. Your language choice is saved only in your own browser (local storage) to remember your preference ; it is never sent to a server. If you write to me by email, your address and message are used only to reply to you. In accordance with the GDPR, you can request access, correction or deletion of your data at any time by email ; note that the statistics are aggregated and cannot be linked back to a person.

Third-party services

The audience measurement service is GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland, with servers in Finland and Germany. Its script is loaded from gc.zgo.at and sends statistics to thibauddrv.goatcounter.com. According to its privacy policy, no information is shared with third parties. The fonts (Inter and Cormorant Garamond) are hosted on this website : no request is sent to Google or any other third party to display them.
